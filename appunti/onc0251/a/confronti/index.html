  • Pyranose = 6-membered ring (5 C + 1 O); furanose = 5-membered ring (4 C + 1 O).
  • Aldose (aldehyde, e.g. glucose) vs ketose (ketone, e.g. fructose).
  • Liver glycogen keeps blood glucose up between meals (12–24 h); muscle glycogen fuels the muscle itself (under 1 h of hard effort).

Mioglobina ed emoglobina

Two almost identical proteins, two jobs
MyoglobinHemoglobin
Chains1 (monomer)4: α₂β₂ (two αβ dimers)
WhereMuscleRed blood cells
JobStores O₂Transports O₂ (and CO₂, H⁺ back)
Binding curveHyperbolicSigmoidal (cooperative)
O₂ affinityHigherLower, and regulated
Regulated by—O₂ (T→R), H⁺ and CO₂ (Bohr effect), 2,3-BPG
T and R states of hemoglobin
T (tense)R (relaxed)
FormDeoxyOxy
O₂ affinityLowHigh
Salt bridges8 presentBroken
Favoured byH⁺, CO₂ (Bohr), 2,3-BPGO₂ binding
  • Fe²⁺ binds O₂; Fe³⁺ = methemoglobin, can't. Heme iron has 6 coordination sites: 4 porphyrin N, 5th = proximal His, 6th = O₂ (empty in deoxy).
  • Distal His forces CO to bind bent: affinity for CO drops about 100× compared with free heme.
  • Bohr effect: lower pH → lower O₂ affinity → more O₂ released where tissues work hard.
  • 2,3-BPG stabilizes T; fetal hemoglobin binds it poorly, so it has higher affinity.
  • Carbonic anhydrase: CO₂ + H₂O → HCO₃⁻ + H⁺; HCO₃⁻ leaves the red cell in exchange for Cl⁻ (chloride shift).
  • Deoxy = high spin (dark red, venous), oxy = low spin (scarlet, arterial).

Enzimi

Pairs to keep apart
PairDifference
Apoenzyme / holoenzymeProtein alone, inactive / protein + cofactor, active
Coenzyme / prosthetic groupBound by weak bonds / bound covalently
Lock-and-key / induced fitRigid active site / active site changes shape on binding
Active site / allosteric siteWhere the reaction happens / another site that changes shape and activity
Changes / does not changeChanges the rate (lowers activation energy) / does not change equilibrium or ΔG
  • An enzyme binds the transition state more tightly than the substrate.
  • High temperature or extreme pH → structure lost (denaturation) → activity lost.
  • Michaelis–Menten: at high [S] the rate approaches Vmax.
  • Phosphorylation: only Ser, Thr, Tyr. Moving a group between molecules = transferase (kinases).
